## Description (997/1000)

ContextFlow is a Safari extension and app for iPhone, iPad and Mac: ask AI about the page you're reading and keep notes, in one chat on every device.

Version 2.5 adds Quick Insert. Tap a text field in Safari and a small ContextFlow button appears beside it. It holds what you copied in Safari, your templates and the latest answer. A template marks what changes, like a name or a date, and fills it in from the page or the conversation you have open.

2.5 also adds: watching a price, stock line or status on a page, with a notification when it changes; reminders to come back to a page; highlights and pins in answers; Recently Deleted for chats.

Who it's for: people who answer the same questions in Safari every day, like shop owners replying to buyers, freelancers, and people who keep an eye on prices.

Why it matters: template tools for the browser mostly run on desktop computers. In Safari on iPad and iPhone nothing inserted right by the field. Priority: high, this is our main update.

## Helpful details (445/500)

Made by one developer. The app and extension are in 11 languages, the App Store page in 15. Buttons and menus have accessibility labels; text size and light or dark appearance are settings. On a Mac and an iPad with a keyboard, Quick Insert works from the keyboard: Control-Shift-V opens the list, Tab moves between blanks. No account. Copied text stays on the device and is erased after 24 hours; templates sync through the person's own iCloud.
