## Description

ContextFlow is a Safari extension and app for iPhone, iPad and Mac: ask AI about the page you're reading and keep notes, in one chat on every device.

Version 2.5 adds price and stock alerts for any store you open in Safari. On a product page, select the price or the stock line and tap the eye, or choose Watch this page… and say what you're waiting for, like "below $150". ContextFlow checks the page while you use Safari and sends a notification when it changes. It works on any shop's site, small ones included, and needs no store account.

The same panel answers questions about the product page: is it worth it, what the reviews keep repeating, and, with Google turned on, whether a newer model is coming. A reminder brings the page back on the day you pick.

Why now: around Black Friday and the holidays people wait for a price on several sites at once. Most price trackers follow one big marketplace or need an account. This works in Safari on whatever store page is open.

## Helpful details

Made by one developer. The app and extension are in 11 languages, the App Store page in 15. Watching a few pages at once is free; Pro watches more. Watching runs while Safari runs, so on iPhone a change can show up once Safari is open again. Buttons and menus have accessibility labels; text size and light or dark appearance are settings. No account needed.
