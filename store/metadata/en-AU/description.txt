ContextFlow puts an AI assistant right inside Safari. Summarise long articles, pull out the key facts, explain anything in plain words or translate the whole page — without copying, pasting or switching apps.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Summarise any article, report, thread or product page in seconds
• Get the key takeaways, numbers and dates at a glance
• Ask your own questions: “What's the catch?”, “Is this worth buying?”, “What should I do next?”
• Explain jargon and complex topics in simple words
• Translate pages into your language
• Select text to explain it, improve your writing or draft a reply

MADE FOR SAFARI ON IPHONE, IPAD AND MAC
• iPhone and iPad: tap the floating ContextFlow button on any page
• Mac: a sidebar that opens from the toolbar or with Control-Shift-E
• Each page keeps its own conversation, so you can come back later
• Light and dark mode, available in 11 languages

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no tracking
• A page is read only when you ask about it
• Page text is never stored on our servers; your chats stay on your devices and sync through your iCloud, end-to-end encrypted

FREE AND PRO
• Free: 5 answers every day
• Pro: up to 375 answers a week on all your Apple devices
• Own key: unlimited answers with your own OpenAI or Claude API key

GET STARTED IN A MINUTE
1. Open the ContextFlow app and follow the steps to turn the extension on in Safari.
2. Open any website and tap the ContextFlow button (on Mac, click the ContextFlow icon in the toolbar).
3. Pick Summarise, Key takeaways, Explain or Translate — or just ask.
