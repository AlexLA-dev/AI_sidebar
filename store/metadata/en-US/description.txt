Stop pasting articles into a chatbot. Ask AI about any page, translate it right where it is and reply with templates that fill themselves, without leaving Safari. The chat follows you from page to page, and the same chat is on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

NEW IN 2.7
• Translate a page right where it is: the text changes in place and links keep working. One tap does the whole site
• Collections: everything for one task, like a trip or a purchase, in one place. In Pro, the AI sorts it into sections, compares cards in a table and sums it all up
• Spaces: keep work, personal and shopping apart, each with its own chats and color
• Check and Rewrite in any field on a site, with the page in mind: shorter, warmer, more formal or more confident
• Add to Calendar or Reminders right from the chat, and keep a snapshot of the page with a note

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Summarize any article, review, thread or product page, pull out the key facts or explain jargon in simple words
• Ask your own questions: “What's the catch?”, “Is this worth buying?”
• Select text to ask about just that part, or keep it as a note
• Google answers: fresh facts from Google Search, with sources you can open
• Templates for replies you write again and again, filled in from the page when you tap a text field
• Find similar: real alternatives to any page, cheaper, newer, in stock or in your language, every link checked
• Listen (Ultra): answers and whole pages read aloud in a natural voice
• Waiting for a price to drop? Select it and tap the eye: ContextFlow tells you when it changes

NOTES AND CHATS
• Notes: swipe the send button up or down (on a Mac, scroll over it) to write a note instead of asking. Notes use no answers
• Whole chat: the answer takes in every message, your notes and all the pages of the chat
• Chats that keep themselves in order (Pro): ContextFlow suggests where a new answer or note belongs and names chats by topic
• Tags, folders and search across your chats

ALL YOUR CHATS IN THE APP
• The ContextFlow app on iPhone, iPad and Mac has every chat and collection from Safari: read it, ask about it or add a note
• Chats and templates sync between your devices through iCloud

MADE FOR SAFARI ON IPHONE, IPAD AND MAC
• iPhone and iPad: tap the floating ContextFlow button on any page, or Ask AI on selected text
• Mac: a sidebar that opens from the toolbar, the floating button or Control-Shift-E
• Tips with short clips, light and dark mode, 15 languages

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no tracking
• A page is read only when you ask about it or watch it
• Page text is never stored on our servers; your chats stay on your devices and sync through your iCloud, end-to-end encrypted

PLANS
• Free: answers every day, a few template fills a day, one collection, and notes with no limit
• Pro: plenty of answers, deeper and more detailed, videos and PDFs, unlimited template fills, collections sorted by AI, more watched pages, AI search and “Ask your chats”
• Ultra: everything in Pro, plus Listen, Find similar that searches wider and up to 50 watched pages
• Own key: unlimited answers with your own OpenAI or Claude API key
• Pro, Ultra and Own key: monthly or yearly

GET STARTED IN A MINUTE
1. Open the ContextFlow app: it shows you step by step how to turn the extension on in Safari.
2. Open any website and tap the ContextFlow button (on a Mac, click the ContextFlow icon in the toolbar).
3. Pick Summarize, Key takeaways, Explain simply or Translate — or just ask.
