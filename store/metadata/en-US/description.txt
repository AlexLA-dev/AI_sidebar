Stop pasting articles into a chatbot. Ask AI about any page, reply with templates that fill themselves and get a notification when a price changes, without leaving Safari. The chat follows you from page to page, and the same chat is on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

NEW IN 2.6
• Find similar (Ultra): real alternatives to any page, cheaper, newer, in stock, free or in your language. Every link is opened and checked before you see it
• Listen (Ultra): answers and whole pages read aloud in a natural voice, in 100+ languages. On iPhone it keeps playing with the screen locked
• Chats that keep themselves in order (Pro): ContextFlow suggests where a new answer or note belongs, a new chat for it or one of your tags, and names chats by topic
• A note in your own words on any reminder or watched price
• Yearly plans for Own key, Pro and Ultra
• A short guide to your first answer, and the panel in Traditional Chinese, Arabic and Vietnamese

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Summarize any article, review, thread or product page, or pull out the key facts
• Ask your own questions: “What's the catch?”, “Is this worth buying?”
• Explain jargon in simple words, or translate the page into your language
• Select text to ask about just that part, or keep it as a note
• Google answers: fresh facts from Google Search, with sources you can open
• Templates for replies you write again and again, filled in from the page. Tap a text field and the floating button brings them, with what you copied
• Waiting for a price to drop? Select it and tap the eye: ContextFlow tells you when it changes. Tap the bell to come back to a page later

NOTES AND CHATS
• Notes: swipe the send button up or down (on a Mac, scroll over it) to write a note instead of asking. Notes use no answers
• Whole chat: the answer takes in every message, your notes and all the pages of the chat
• Tags, folders and search across your chats
• Copy, share or tag any answer or note, or share the whole chat

ALL YOUR CHATS IN THE APP
• The ContextFlow app on iPhone, iPad and Mac has every chat from Safari: read it, ask about it or add a note
• Chats and templates sync between your devices through iCloud

MADE FOR SAFARI ON IPHONE, IPAD AND MAC
• iPhone and iPad: tap the floating ContextFlow button on any page, or Ask AI on selected text
• Mac: a sidebar that opens from the toolbar, the floating button or Control-Shift-E
• Tips with short clips, light and dark mode, 14 languages

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no tracking
• A page is read only when you ask about it or watch it
• Page text is never stored on our servers; your chats stay on your devices and sync through your iCloud, end-to-end encrypted

PLANS

GET STARTED IN A MINUTE
1. Open the ContextFlow app: it shows you step by step how to turn the extension on in Safari.
2. Open any website and tap the ContextFlow button (on a Mac, click the ContextFlow icon in the toolbar).
3. Pick Summarize, Key takeaways, Explain simply or Translate — or just ask.
