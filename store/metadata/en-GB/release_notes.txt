What's new in ContextFlow 2.3:
• Notes: swipe the send button up or down (on a Mac, scroll on it) to write a note instead of asking. A note stays in the chat with the page it's about, syncs with it and uses no answers.
• Whole chat: turn it on under the question field and the answer takes in everything — every message, your notes and the other pages of the chat.
• Chats: keep several conversations at once, pin the important ones and drag them into order, rename them, and see every page a chat was on — tap one to go back to it.
• Pick up where you left off: chats sync across your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your iCloud, end-to-end encrypted, and never touch our servers. You can turn this off in Settings.
• Share just what you need: tick the messages to send — their links and sources go with them.
• A short tour the first time you open ContextFlow, and Tips in the app with a short video of each feature
• Your Pro answer count stays up to date across devices
• Fixes and improvements
