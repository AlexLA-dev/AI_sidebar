Arc Search lost Browse for Me? Here's Safari on iPhone

Updated November 2026 · from the developer of ContextFlow

Arc's site says it plainly: on November 23, 2026, “on iOS and Android, Browse for Me and other AI features will be disabled” (arc.net). Arc Search keeps working as a lite browser, but you can no longer sign in or sync your tabs. If you're moving to Safari, here's what it can do instead and what's different. I make ContextFlow, one of the options below, so keep that in mind.

What changes in Arc Search

  • November 23, 2026: Browse for Me and the other AI features turn off, and sign-in and sync stop. The app stays as a lite browser.
  • Your open tabs: to keep a list of them, tap the Tab Switcher icon in the bottom-left corner, tap Settings, then Export Data. You can share or copy the list.
  • Dia, the team's replacement for Arc, runs on Mac and Windows. Its site doesn't mention an iPhone app.

What Safari on iPhone does on its own

  • Search from the address bar, with the search engine you choose in Safari's settings.
  • Summarize in Reader, on iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro and later).
  • What it doesn't do: build one answer for you from several sites, the way Browse for Me did.

What ContextFlow adds to Safari

  • Answers from Google, with sources: turn on Google next to the question field and ask. The answer comes from Google Search, with the sites it used listed under it to open.
  • The gist of the page: tap the ContextFlow button on any page, then Summarize or Key takeaways. Or ask your own question about the page, or about a part you select.
  • Find similar Ultra: on a product, an app, an article or a recipe, it looks for alternatives that are cheaper, newer, in stock, free or in your language, and opens every page it finds to check it before you see it. 10 searches a week.
  • Your reading stays together: answers, notes and the pages they came from are in chats on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, synced through your iCloud. No account.

What's honestly different

  • It starts from a page, not from the search box. Browse for Me took a search and gave you a new page. ContextFlow works on a web page you've opened, in a panel next to it. Safari doesn't let extensions run on its start page, so open any page first.
  • It isn't a search engine. If you used Browse for Me instead of searching, an AI search app is closer to it than any Safari extension.
  • Free has a daily allowance of answers. Pro gives you more, and Find similar is Ultra only.

Questions

When does Browse for Me stop working?

On November 23, 2026. Arc's site says Browse for Me and other AI features turn off in Arc Search on iOS and Android that day. The app keeps working as a lite browser, without sign-in or sync.

How do I keep my open tabs from Arc Search?

Tap the Tab Switcher icon in the bottom-left corner, tap Settings, then Export Data. You get a list of your open tabs to share or copy.

Is ContextFlow a browser?

No. It's an extension inside Safari, plus an app that has all your chats. The app shows you how to turn it on in Safari, and then it's on every web page you open.
