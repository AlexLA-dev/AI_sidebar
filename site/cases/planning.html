Cases › Planning over days

Planning something that takes more than one evening?

A trip, a move, a new job, a renovation. You read for days, across dozens of pages, and by the end you can't remember which hotel had the good breakfast or why you ruled out the second flat.

Bookmarks keep the pages, not what you thought about them. This is how I'd use ContextFlow for it.

One chat for the whole plan

Start a chat for the trip and keep asking as you go from page to page. The chat remembers which page each question was about, and lists them all. Tap a page's name to go back to it.

Chats that follow you, free.

The small print that matters

The cancellation policy, the baggage rules, the notice period. Select that paragraph and ask: “Can I cancel for free?” The answer is about that part only.

Questions about a selection, free.

Write down what you liked

Have a thought, not a question? Swipe the send button up or down (on Mac, scroll on it) to write a note. Or select a line on the page and tap Note. Notes stay in the chat next to their page and don't use answers.

Notes, free.

Decide with everything in front of you

Turn on Whole chat and ask “Which of these three flats fits what I wrote I need?” The answer uses every page, answer and note in the chat, so it weighs your own words too.

Whole chat, free.

Come back to it next week

Select the line that matters, or take the whole page, tap the bell, write what to check, and pick a time: the reminder brings you back to the page and says your note. Tag the chat, or turn on Folders, to keep the trip apart from work. Search finds a chat by any word in it. With iCloud on, the same chat is on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Reminders, tags, folders, search, free. AI search, for when you remember the topic but not the words, Pro

What it isn't

  • It isn't a bookmark manager or a web clipper: it doesn't save copies of pages. It keeps the links, your notes and the answers in a chat.
  • Notes go to the AI only when you ask with Whole chat on. Otherwise they stay on your devices and in your iCloud.
  • More on notes in How to keep notes while reading in Safari.

What's free, what's Pro

Free

Chats across pages, notes, Whole chat, tags, folders, search and reminders, with a daily allowance of answers.

Pro Pro

AI search over your chats, questions over all your chats, suggestions that sort messages into the right chats, merge chats and name them by topic, and more answers.

Questions

Can I share the plan with someone?

Yes. Share one answer or the whole chat. The text comes with its links and its sources.

Does it work across my devices?

Turn on Sync chats with iCloud, and a chat started on iPhone continues on your iPad and Mac. It's end-to-end encrypted in your iCloud and never stored on my server.

Is it free?

Chats, notes, tags, folders, search and reminders are free. Questions use answers, with a daily allowance on Free. AI search and questions over all your chats are Pro.
