Cases › Replies and forms

Typing the same replies every day?

If you answer people for a living, in sales, recruiting or support, most of your typing is the same few messages with a different name and date. The rest is carrying things from one tab into a form in another.

Quick insert in ContextFlow is for that. It works in any text field on a web page in Safari, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

You copied it three tabs ago, then copied over it

An order number, then a tracking link, then an address, and now you need the order number again. Tap the text field, then the ContextFlow button next to it. The last things you copied on pages in Safari are in the list, and one tap puts one in.

Copied, free. It keeps things for a day, on this device only. Nothing from password or card fields, and nothing in Private Browsing.

The same reply, only the name and the date change

A candidate asks to move the call. A customer asks when the order ships. Select a reply you've already sent and tap Template: ContextFlow marks what changes. Next time, insert it and tap Fill from the page. The name and the date come from the conversation you have open, highlighted so you can check them before you send.

Templates, free. Fill from the page: a few times a day on Free, as often as you need with Pro

Forms you fill in every week

Company details, a delivery address, the standard description of what you sell. Save each one as a template once, and it's one tap away in any field on any site. With a keyboard on iPad or Mac, type / and a few words of the template, then press Tab. On Mac, Control-Shift-V opens the whole list. Templates sync through your iCloud, so the ones you make on Mac are on your iPhone too.

Templates and the button by the field, free.

A reply that depends on the page

Someone sent a long message, or you're answering about a listing or a document. Open ContextFlow on that page and ask for a draft: “a short, polite no”, “answer her three questions”. Under the answer, Insert into field puts it into the reply box. Read it before you send it.

Answers and Insert into field. Free to start; if you ask a lot, Pro gives you more answers.

Where it won't help

  • It lives in Safari. What you copy in other apps doesn't show up in Copied, and in Mail or Messages there's no ContextFlow button.
  • It isn't a keyboard or a clipboard for the whole phone. If that's what you need, this isn't it.
  • A few fields don't accept text from extensions. Then ContextFlow copies it and tells you, and you paste it yourself.

Questions

Does it see everything I copy?

No. Only what you copy on web pages in Safari. It skips password and card fields and Private Browsing, keeps things for a day on this device only, and you can clear the list or turn Copied off in settings.

Where are my templates kept?

On your devices, and in your iCloud if sync is on. When you tap Fill from the page, the template and the page's text go to my server to find the values. Nothing is kept there.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. Type / and a few words of a template in any field, then press Tab, or press Control-Shift-V to open the list.

Is it free?

Copied, templates and inserting are free. Filling blanks from the page works a few times a day on Free, and as often as you need with Pro.
