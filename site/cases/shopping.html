Cases › Shopping

Not sure it's worth the money?

You've found the thing. Now there are hundreds of reviews, a spec list you half understand, and a price that might drop next week.

ContextFlow works on the product page itself, on any store's site you open in Safari.

Worth buying? What's the catch?

Ask exactly that on the product page. The answer comes from what's on it: the description, the specs and the reviews you can see. You get what's good, the catch, the price and a plain verdict.

Questions about the page, free to start.

What do the reviews really say?

Instead of scrolling through all of them, ask for what people repeat. You get it side by side: what's fine, and what keeps coming up, like ears getting warm or a bulky case.

Questions about the page. It reads the reviews on the page you have open.

Is a newer model coming?

Some questions aren't answered on the page. Turn on Google next to the question field, and the answer comes from Google Search with its sources: a newer version, a known problem, what changed since last year.

Answers from Google, free.

Is there a cheaper one, or one in stock?

Tap Find similar on the product page and pick Cheaper, In stock or Newer, or say what you need, like “same size, under $100”. You get alternatives from other shops, each page opened and checked, with the price and stock it shows. Watch price follows any of them.

Find similar Ultra

You'll buy it, just not at this price

Select the price or the stock status and tap the eye. Or choose Watch this page… in the … menu at the top of the panel, and say what you're waiting for, like “below $150”. Add a note to yourself if you like, such as “only the black one”. ContextFlow tells you when it changes.

Watching a page, free for a few pages at once. Pro watches more.

Decide after payday

Not ready to choose? Select a line and tap the bell, or choose Remind me about this page… in the … menu, and pick a time. The reminder opens this page.

Reminders, free.

Good to know

  • It reads what's on the page. It can't tell you which reviews are fake, and it doesn't see reviews on other sites unless you open them.
  • Watching runs while Safari runs. On iPhone you may hear about a change once you're back in Safari, not the minute it happens. A few sites don't let it check in the background; then it checks when you open the page.
  • It works on any store you open in Safari, not just one shop, and needs no store account.

Questions

Does it work on any store?

On store sites you open in Safari, yes, small shops included. It isn't tied to one marketplace.

Can it spot fake reviews?

No. It sums up what the reviews on the page say and what people repeat. It can't tell which ones are real.

Can it find the same thing cheaper somewhere else?

With Ultra, Find similar looks for alternatives that are cheaper, in stock or newer, opens every page it finds and checks it before showing it to you. It isn't a price comparison of every shop, and it's 10 searches a week.

How do I hear about a price drop?

As a notification from ContextFlow. If notifications are off for it, the panel tells you how to turn them on.

Is it free?

Reminders and watching a few pages at once are free. Questions use answers: Free has a daily allowance, Pro gives you more.
