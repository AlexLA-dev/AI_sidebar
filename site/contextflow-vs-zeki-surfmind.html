ContextFlow, Zeki or SurfMind: three AI sidebars for Safari

Updated November 2026 · from the developer of ContextFlow

All three put an AI chat next to the page you're reading in Safari, and all three can summarize it, answer questions about it and work with selected text. They differ in what comes on top. I make ContextFlow, so I'm not neutral. Zeki and SurfMind are described here as their App Store pages describe them, in November 2026.

Zeki

  • A sidebar in Safari on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and a chat app.
  • Your own key: free and unlimited, with keys from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter, Groq or Mistral, and any model they offer.
  • Without a key: 30 free messages a week, or a Zeki Plus or Pro subscription (200 or 1,000 messages a day) after Sign in with Apple.
  • Compares two models side by side, reads files, PDFs and images, searches the web, keeps a knowledge base.
  • Pick it when: you want to choose your model and already have an API key.

SurfMind

  • Its App Store page lists iPhone and Mac.
  • 100+ models with your own keys, trial credits to start, and local models through Ollama, LM Studio and others.
  • Compares information across several open tabs, and can click, scroll and type on pages to do a task for you.
  • Pick it when: you want local models, many tabs at once, or an assistant that acts on pages.

ContextFlow

  • Safari on iPhone, iPad and Mac. No account: it works right after you turn it on.
  • Without a key: 5 answers a day free, more with Pro or Ultra. Answers come from Anthropic's Claude, and from OpenAI when Claude is unavailable. You don't pick the model.
  • Your own key: the Own key plan, with an OpenAI or Claude key.
  • On top of chat:
    • translates a whole page in place, links still working;
    • checks and rewrites what you type on websites, with the page in mind;
    • Listen reads answers and whole pages aloud (Ultra);
    • Find similar finds cheaper, in-stock or newer alternatives and checks each page (Pro, Own key and Ultra);
    • Watch price tells you when a page's price changes;
    • your notes, chats and collections stay with the pages they're about.
  • Pick it when: you don't want accounts or keys, and you read, shop and write in Safari more than you research with many models.
  • It has no website blocker or focus mode. A Chrome extension with that name does; it's a different app.

Questions

Which one is best?

It depends on what you do. Zeki if you have an API key and want to pick models. SurfMind for local models and an assistant that acts on pages. ContextFlow if you want no account and no key, and want translating, checking, Listen and Find similar right in Safari.

Is ContextFlow free?

Yes, to start: 5 answers a day, no account. Pro and Ultra give more answers and more features. The Own key plan uses your own OpenAI or Claude key.

Is ContextFlow the website blocker for Chrome?

No. That's a different app with a similar name. This ContextFlow is “ContextFlow: AI for Safari” on the App Store, for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Can I try all three?

Yes. All three are free to start in the App Store, and Safari lets you turn extensions on and off one by one.
