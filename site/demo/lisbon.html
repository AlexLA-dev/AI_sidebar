Lisbon in three days: what's really worth it

Trams, viewpoints and custard tarts: an easy route to see the essentials without rushing around.

By Lucía Ferrer · 7 min read

On day one, walk up to Alfama and leave tram 28 for the afternoon, when the line is shorter. From the Santa Luzia viewpoint you can see the whole neighborhood down to the river.

For lunch, find a small tasca with a set menu and order grilled sardines: in summer they're almost a must.

Day two: Belém

Get to the Jerónimos Monastery early; by ten the tour groups are there. The Belém Tower is then a fifteen-minute walk along the river.

Don't leave without trying the custard tarts from the old bakery. If the line is long, it moves fast: buy half a dozen and eat them in the garden across the street.

Day three: Sintra

Take the train from Rossio first thing and book your Pena Palace ticket ahead: in high season the morning slots sell out.
