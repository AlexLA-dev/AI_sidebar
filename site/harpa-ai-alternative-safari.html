HARPA AI in Safari: what to use instead

Updated November 2026 · from the developer of ContextFlow

HARPA AI is an extension for Chrome and other Chromium browsers: it answers questions about the page, watches prices and runs automations. As of November 2026 it has no Safari version and no iPhone app. I make ContextFlow, a Safari extension that covers part of what HARPA does, so keep that in mind. Here's what carries over and what doesn't.

What HARPA AI does

  • AI on the page: summaries and answers about the page, videos and PDFs, with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
  • Watching pages and prices: it checks as often as every 15 seconds or as rarely as once a month, every 3 hours by default. The checks run in a minimized browser window, and an alert can be a browser notification, an email or a webhook (HARPA's guide).
  • Commands and automation: 100+ ready-made prompts you can edit, and automation that clicks, fills in forms and pulls data from sites.
  • Where and how much: Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers on a computer. Free gives 10 messages a day and 100 command runs in all; paid plans start at $12 a month, billed yearly (HARPA's pricing).

What ContextFlow does in Safari

  • Ask about the page you're on, or a part you select: Summarize, Key takeaways, Explain simply, Translate, or your own question. Turn on Google for fresh facts, with sources you can open.
  • Watch a price or the stock: select it and tap the eye, or say what you're waiting for, like “below $150”. You get a notification when it changes. More in shopping in Safari.
  • Templates: replies with blanks for the name or the date, filled in from the page you have open and put into the field you're typing in. More in replies and forms.
  • Everywhere you use Safari: iPhone, iPad and Mac. Chats and templates sync through your iCloud, and there's no account to make.

Where HARPA AI is still better

  • Automation. HARPA clicks, fills in forms, pulls data across sites and runs commands on a schedule. ContextFlow doesn't automate anything.
  • Watching. HARPA checks as often as every 15 seconds while your computer's browser runs, and can email you or call a webhook. ContextFlow checks a page at most every 15 minutes, only while Safari runs, and tells you with a notification.
  • Models. HARPA lets you pick ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and bring your own keys on its lifetime plan. ContextFlow picks the model for you; with the Own key plan you can use your own OpenAI or Claude key.
  • Other browsers. HARPA works in Chrome, Edge and Brave on a computer. ContextFlow is Safari only.

Where ContextFlow is the better pick

  • You read on an iPhone or an iPad. HARPA has nothing there; ContextFlow works in Safari on both.
  • You use Safari on a Mac and don't want a second browser for one extension.
  • You want answers, your notes and price alerts to stay with the pages they're about, in one chat per topic, on all your devices.

Questions

Is there a HARPA AI extension for Safari?

Not as of November 2026. HARPA's site covers Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers, and I couldn't find a HARPA app in the App Store.

Can ContextFlow watch prices like HARPA does?

Yes, on any store page you open in Safari: select the price or the stock status and tap the eye. It checks while Safari runs, at most every 15 minutes per page, and sends a notification. Free watches 5 pages at once, Pro 20 and Ultra 50.

Does ContextFlow fill in forms or click for me?

No. It can put a template or an answer into the field you're typing in, and you send it yourself.
