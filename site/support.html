Getting started

How do I turn on ContextFlow on iPhone or iPad?

Open the Settings app → Apps → Safari → Extensions → ContextFlow, switch it on and set All Websites to Allow. Then open any website in Safari and tap the ✦ button (or the page menu in the address bar → ContextFlow).

How do I turn it on on Mac?

Open Safari → Settings → Extensions and tick ContextFlow, then allow it on all websites. Click the ContextFlow icon in the toolbar or press Control-Shift-E on any page.

Nothing happens when I tap the button

Make sure ContextFlow is allowed on the site (Safari's page menu → ContextFlow → Always Allow on Every Website). Some pages can't be read by extensions (Safari's start page, App Store pages); open a regular website instead. Reloading the page after enabling the extension also helps.

Plans and subscriptions

How many free answers do I get?

5 answers every day, renewed at midnight UTC. No account is needed.

I subscribed, but ContextFlow still shows the free plan

Open the ContextFlow app once (it shares your subscription with the extension), then reopen the panel in Safari. If needed, tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen.

Does one subscription work on all my devices?

Yes — on every iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. Open the ContextFlow app once on each device.

How do I cancel?

iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ContextFlow. Mac: App Store → your name → Account Settings → Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

How do I use my own OpenAI or Claude key?

With the Pro or Own key plan, open the ContextFlow panel in Safari → Settings → Own API key and paste an OpenAI key (sk-…) or an Anthropic key (sk-ant-…). Under Model, pick Fast (newest Claude Haiku or GPT mini) or Smart (newest Claude Sonnet or GPT). Requests then go directly from your device to that provider.

Privacy

What is sent when I ask a question?

Your question and the page text (or your selection) — only at that moment, only to generate the answer. Details in the Privacy Policy.

I had an account in an older version. How do I delete it?

E-mail us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it within 30 days. Version 2.0 no longer uses accounts.
