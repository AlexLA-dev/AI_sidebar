Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enable the extension in Safari?

Open Safari → Settings → Extensions → enable ContextFlow. On iPhone, tap the puzzle piece icon in Safari's address bar and enable ContextFlow.

The extension doesn't see my subscription

Open the ContextFlow app and check the Status tab. Make sure "Subscription active" shows OK. If not, try tapping "Restore Purchases" on the Subscription tab.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your device Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → ContextFlow → Cancel. You'll keep access until the end of the billing period.

How do I use my own OpenAI API key?

Subscribe to the BYOK plan ($1.99/mo), then open the ContextFlow app → Settings → enter your API key. The extension will use it automatically.

Can I use ContextFlow on both Mac and iPhone?

Yes! Your subscription works across all your Apple devices. Just sign in with the same account in the extension on each device.
