Ask AI about the page you're reading in Safari, on iPhone and iPad

Updated November 2026 · from the developer of ContextFlow

You're halfway through an article, a contract or a long forum thread, and you have a question about it. There are three ways to ask AI about it in Safari on iPhone and iPad. I make one of them, ContextFlow, so weigh that.

Share the page to the ChatGPT app

  • Tap Share in Safari and choose ChatGPT, or copy the link and paste it into a chat. Then ask.
  • Good: strong models, a free tier, and room for a long conversation.
  • Limits: you switch apps for every question. With only the link, ChatGPT opens the page on its own, so it can't see pages you're signed in to or that sit behind a paywall. And the chat lives in ChatGPT, not next to the page.

Safari's own summary

  • On devices with Apple Intelligence, open Reader from the address bar and tap Summarize.
  • Good: free and built in.
  • Limits: it gives you a summary, not answers to your own questions. It needs Apple Intelligence in a supported language and region, and a page that opens in Reader.

Asking on the page, with ContextFlow

  • Tap ✦ at the edge of the screen, or open the page menu in the address bar and choose ContextFlow. Ask your question.
  • It reads the page as it is in your Safari, so a page you're signed in to works too.
  • Only one paragraph is unclear? Select it and ask about that part only, or tap Summarize, Translate and more under the selection.
  • A chat carries on from page to page and keeps the pages, your questions, the answers and your own notes together.
  • On iPad it's the same ✦ as on iPhone, and you can drag the panel by its top bar. On Mac it's a sidebar: the toolbar icon or Control-Shift-E.

Where it falls short

  • Safari only.
  • Safari doesn't let extensions read a few pages, like the start page and App Store pages. You can still ask general questions there.
  • Answers come from an AI model online, so you need a connection.
  • Free has 5 answers a day. If you ask a lot, you'll want Pro.

Questions

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. On iPad it's the same ✦ as on iPhone, and the panel can be dragged by its top bar. On Mac it opens as a sidebar.

Do I need an account?

No. ContextFlow works right after you turn it on in Safari. A subscription is tied to your Apple Account.

Is the page sent anywhere?

Only when you ask something: your question and the page's text go to the AI model to write the answer. Page text isn't kept on my server and isn't used to train models.

Why don't I see ✦?

Usually Safari is still waiting for you to allow the extension on websites. Here is how to turn it on.
