Leaving Arc: Arc Max's AI in Safari

Updated November 2026 · from the developer of ContextFlow

Arc stops working on December 22, 2026, and Browse for Me and the other AI features in Arc Search on iPhone turn off on November 23. The Browser Company points people to Dia. If you're going back to Safari instead, here's what you lose from Arc Max and what you can get back. I make ContextFlow, one of the options below, so weigh that.

What's happening to Arc

  • No new Arc accounts since October 22, 2026. Arc for Mac and Windows stops working on December 22, 2026 (arc.net).
  • From November 23, Arc Search on iPhone keeps working as a plain browser, without sign-in, sync or its AI features.
  • Dia, from the same team, is the replacement. It runs on Mac and Windows, and its AI features come with paid plans, from $10 a month (Dia's pricing).

What ContextFlow adds to Safari

  • Questions about the page you're on, or about a paragraph you select, with follow-ups. Translate and Explain simply are one tap.
  • Chats that keep your reading together: answers, your notes and the pages they came from, on iPhone, iPad and Mac, synced through your iCloud.
  • Extras Arc didn't have: price and stock alerts, reminders to come back to a page, and templates for replies you type again and again.
  • On a Mac it opens as a sidebar from the toolbar, the floating button or Control-Shift-E.

What you won't get back

  • Link previews, tidy tab titles and tidy downloads. Neither Safari nor ContextFlow does them.
  • Arc itself. Spaces, the sidebar of tabs and Arc's own layout aren't something an extension can bring to Safari.
  • AI across many tabs and your work tools. If that's what you used most, Dia on a Mac or Windows PC is the closer pick.

Questions

When does Arc stop working?

On December 22, 2026, on Mac and Windows, according to arc.net. Arc Search's AI features on iPhone turn off on November 23, 2026; the app keeps working as a browser.

Is ContextFlow a browser like Arc or Dia?

No. It's an extension inside Safari, plus an app that has all your chats. You keep Safari, with its tab groups and your passwords.

Does ContextFlow work on Windows?

No. It works in Safari on iPhone, iPad and Mac only.
