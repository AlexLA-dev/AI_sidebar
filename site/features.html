What ContextFlow can do

These are the same tips as in the app, grouped by what you're trying to do. Each clip is the real panel at work. Cards marked Pro need the Pro plan. Everything else works on Free.

On all your devices

Started on iPhone, finishing on Mac? Turn on Sync chats with iCloud, and a chat continues on your iPad and Mac. It's end-to-end encrypted in your iCloud and never stored on my server.

Get started

Open it on the page you're reading and ask. No prompts to write.

Open it on any page

Where is it? On iPhone and iPad, tap ✦ on the page, or open the page menu in the address bar and choose ContextFlow. On Mac, click ✦ or the ContextFlow icon in the toolbar, or press Control-Shift-E.

One tap for the gist

Long article and no time? Summarize, Key takeaways, Explain simply and Translate work on the whole page. You don't have to write a prompt.

Ask about a part

Only one paragraph is confusing? Select it and choose Ask AI. The answer is about that part only. It can also explain, translate or rewrite it.

Ask or note a selection

Found a line worth keeping? Select it and tap Note: it goes into the chat with its page. Or type your own question about it right under the selection. Notes don't use answers.

A chat follows you

Reading across several pages? Keep asking as you go. The chat remembers the conversation and which page each question was about. Tap a page's name in the chat to go back to it.

Fresh facts from Google

Need something recent? Turn on Google next to the question field, and the answer comes from Google Search with its sources: news, prices, anything new.

Every day

The small things that turn a chat into a notebook for what you read.

Notes of your own

Have a thought, not a question? Swipe the send button up or down (on Mac, scroll on it) to write a note instead. It stays in the chat with its page and doesn't use answers.

Highlight what matters

One line in an answer is the one you need? Select it and tap Highlight. It stays marked, like with a pen. Later answers in the chat take your highlights into account, and Copy keeps them in bold.

Pinned at the top

Lost a good answer in a long chat? Pin it, and it waits in the bar at the top of the chat. Tap the bar to jump to it, tap again for the next one.

Swipe to pin or delete

On iPhone and iPad, swipe an answer or a note to the right to pin it, to the left to delete it. On Mac, point at it and the same buttons show beside it. Changed your mind? Undo brings it back.

The whole chat in mind

Want an answer that uses everything you collected? Turn on Whole chat under the question field: every message, your notes and the other pages of this chat go with the question. Off, it's the last messages and the open page.

Ask about a video Pro

Don't want to watch the whole thing to find one part? ContextFlow reads the video's transcript. Ask for the gist, or what was said about something, and the answer points to the moment, like [4:15]. On Free, answers use the page's text.

PDFs on the page Pro

The details are in a PDF? PDFs shown on the page or linked from it go with your question, and the answer says which page of the PDF it's from. On iPad and Mac it works on a PDF tab too. On Free, answers use the page's text.

Quick insert

For forms, replies and anything you keep typing again.

Insert into any field

Typing the same things into forms? Tap a text field on a page, then the ContextFlow button by it: what you copied in other tabs, your templates and the last answer are one tap away. With a keyboard, type / and a few words of a template, then Tab. On Mac, Control-Shift-V opens the whole list.

Copied, kept for a day

Copied something three tabs ago, then copied over it? The last things you copied on pages in Safari wait in the list for a day, on this device only. Nothing from password or card fields, and nothing in Private Browsing.

Templates that fill themselves

Same reply every time, only the name and the date change? Select it and tap Template: ContextFlow marks what changes. When you insert it, Fill from the page puts them in, highlighted so you can check.

Watch and remind

The bell is about time: come back when you choose. The eye is about a change: hear about it when it happens.

Remind me about this page

Want to finish reading tonight? Select a line and tap the bell, or tap … at the top of the panel, then Remind me about this page…, and pick a time. The reminder opens this page. Answers and notes have Remind me in their … menu.

Know when a price drops

Waiting for a sale or for something to be back in stock? Select the price, the stock status or a date and tap the eye. For the whole page, choose Watch this page… in the … menu at the top of the panel. ContextFlow checks the page while you use Safari and tells you when it changes, or drops below the price you name.

Chats in order

Find an old chat, keep topics apart, take your chats to your other devices.

Start fresh or go back

New topic? Start a new chat with +. Earlier chats wait in the chat list: open one to pick up where you left off, rename it with the pencil, or delete it.

Search your chats

Remember a word but not the chat? Type it in the search over the chat list: every chat with it shows up, with the place it's in, and a result opens the chat right there.

Tags and folders

Work and home in one list? Tag a chat, an answer or a note with 🏷. Tap a tag to see only its chats, or turn on Folders to group the list by tag.

Share what you found

Sending it to someone? Share one answer or the whole chat. The text comes with its links, its sources and a link to ContextFlow.

Your chats in the app

Want to read a chat away from Safari? The ContextFlow app has every chat. Ask about its pages or add a note right there, tag, copy or share any answer. Continue in Safari picks it up where you left off.

AI search Pro

Remember what a chat was about, but none of its words? Tap ✦ next to the search and describe it, like “that article about sleep and coffee”.

Ask your chats Pro

“What did I read this week?” The first row of the chat list answers from all your chats at once. Try “Summarize my notes on AI”.

Where it belongs Pro

Asked about a car in the chat about a sofa? Under the answer, ContextFlow suggests the chat it belongs in, or a new one. One tap moves it there; Undo puts it back.

Your tags, suggested Pro

Tired of tagging by hand? ContextFlow learns what your tags mean from where you put them, and suggests one under a new answer or note. Only your own tags, and only when one fits.

Privacy & personalization

Make it fit you, and know what leaves your device.

Tell it about you

Want answers that fit you? Add a line under About me, like what you do or how you like answers, and ContextFlow takes it into account.

The ✦ button, your way

Is the ✦ button in the way on some site? Hide it on that site, or turn it off everywhere in the extension settings. ContextFlow still opens from Safari's extensions menu.

Use your own API key

Have an OpenAI or Claude key? With Pro or the Own key plan, paste it in the extension settings in Safari, and requests go straight to that provider.

Private by design

What gets sent? ContextFlow reads a page only when you ask, and sends just its text to get the answer. Your chats stay on your devices and in your iCloud. With suggestions on, the text of new messages also goes to my server to find where they belong; I don't keep it.
