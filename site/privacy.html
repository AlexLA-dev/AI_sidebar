Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · applies to ContextFlow 2.0 and later

In short: ContextFlow has no accounts, no tracking and no ads. We don't store the pages, questions or chats you send to an AI feature. Your chats stay on your devices and, if you sync them, in your own iCloud, which we can't access.

1. Who we are

ContextFlow ("we", "us") is a Safari extension with a companion app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. You can reach us at top10resource@gmail.com.

2. What we keep

We keep only what we need to run ContextFlow, keep it working and improve it. None of it is linked to your name, e-mail address or Apple Account.

  • Random IDs for your installation, created by ContextFlow, with your device type, app version and language.
  • Usage records: which features you use and how often, with sizes, timings and costs. They never contain the pages you visit, their text or your chats. The only words they can contain are short settings you give a feature, such as the condition you set for Find similar.
  • Your subscription status from the App Store: the plan, its dates, the price paid and the App Store country. We never see your payment details.
  • If you came to ContextFlow from an Apple Ads ad on the App Store: which campaign it was, as Apple reports it, without telling us who you are.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address that changes every day, to prevent abuse.
  • On this website: which kind of source a visit came from and which of our pages it was. No cookies.

We keep usage, ad and website records for up to 180 days, subscription records while the subscription is active and for up to 180 days after, and your installation IDs until you ask us to delete them.

3. What goes to AI providers

When you use an AI feature, the content that request needs goes to one of our AI providers. Depending on the feature, that's your question or other words you type, the page or the text you selected, your templates, excerpts of your chats, the text you want read aloud, and your "About me" text if you wrote one. ContextFlow's AI features include answers (including Google answers and "Ask your chats"), AI search, "Fill from the page" and "Find by meaning" for templates, Page watch, Suggestions (only if you turn them on), Listen and Find similar.

We don't store this content, apart from the short settings described in section 2. Our AI providers keep it only for a limited time, for safety and abuse monitoring, and don't use it to train their models.

If you use your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key, answers go straight from your device to that provider, under your own account with them, and don't pass through our servers. We only receive usage counts.

4. On your devices and in your iCloud

Your chats, templates, settings, your "About me" text and your own API key are kept on your device, along with what ContextFlow stores there for its features, such as text you copied, pages you watch and audio you listened to.

With chat sync on, your chats and templates, and from ContextFlow 2.6 the audio you listened to, are also kept in your private iCloud so you can continue on your other devices. There they are protected by Apple's iCloud encryption. Syncing goes between your devices and iCloud and never through our servers, and we have no access to your iCloud.

"Delete all chats" in the settings removes your chats and saved audio from that device and, with chat sync on, from your iCloud. Saved audio also expires after 7 days. Removing the app deletes what it keeps on that device.

5. What we don't do

  • No accounts, e-mail addresses or passwords.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no ads in ContextFlow.
  • We don't collect your browsing history.
  • We don't sell your data or share it for marketing.

6. Service providers

  • Anthropic — AI (Claude) (privacy policy).
  • OpenAI — AI (privacy policy).
  • Google — AI (Gemini), Google answers and read-aloud voices (privacy policy).
  • Netlify — hosts our server and this website (privacy policy).
  • Supabase — our database for the records in section 2 (privacy policy).
  • Apple — App Store payments, Apple Ads and iCloud (privacy policy).

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States.

7. Why we may process it

We process data to provide ContextFlow as you ask us to, and keep usage records because we have a legitimate interest in keeping ContextFlow working and improving it.

8. Your rights

You can ask what we keep about your installation or subscription, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use, by e-mailing top10resource@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have more rights, which we honour, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

9. Children

ContextFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

10. Earlier versions

Versions before 2.0 asked some users to create an account with an e-mail address. ContextFlow no longer uses those accounts. To have yours deleted, e-mail us from that address and we'll delete it within 30 days.

11. Changes

We'll post changes on this page and update the date above.
