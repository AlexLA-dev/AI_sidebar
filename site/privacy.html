Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · applies to ContextFlow 2.0 and later

In short: ContextFlow has no accounts and no tracking. When you ask a question, your question and the text of the page (or the text you selected) are sent to our AI provider to generate the answer and are not stored by us. Your chats stay on your devices and, if you turn on chat sync, in your own iCloud, end-to-end encrypted.

1. Who we are

ContextFlow ("we") is a Safari extension with a companion app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Contact: top10resource@gmail.com.

3. What we don't do

  • No accounts, e-mail addresses or passwords.
  • No browsing history: pages are read only when you open ContextFlow and ask something.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or sharing of data for marketing.

4. Data on your device

Chats, settings, the optional "About me" text and your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key (if you add one) are stored locally in Safari's extension storage. "About me" text is included in requests to personalize answers. With your own key, requests go directly from your device to the provider of that key (OpenAI or Anthropic) under your own account with them, and do not pass through our servers. After such an answer the extension sends us only the usage counters listed in section 2, never your key, your question or the page text. You can delete all chats in the extension settings; removing the app deletes everything.

Chat sync (ContextFlow 2.3 and later). With "Sync chats with iCloud" on in the extension settings (the default), your chats (up to the 50 most recent) are also kept in your private iCloud database so you can continue them on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. They are stored end-to-end encrypted with keys only your devices have: neither we nor Apple can read it, and they never pass through our servers. Turning the setting off stops syncing; deleting a chat, or "Delete all chats", also deletes it from iCloud.

Suggestions (ContextFlow 2.5 and later). With suggestions on, ContextFlow keeps on each of your devices a numeric representation (embedding) of each message, so it compares messages on the device instead of sending your chats anywhere; only the text of new messages is sent, as described in section 2. These numbers are not synced to iCloud and are deleted with your chats. Turning a suggestion down, or taking it, is saved with the message and syncs with the chat. Suggestions are off until you turn them on, in the extension's or the app's settings.

Copied and templates (ContextFlow 2.5 and later). "Copied" keeps the last 5 pieces of text you copy on web pages in Safari, and what you copy with ContextFlow's own Copy button, so you can insert them into another field. They stay on your device only: they are never sent to us, to AI providers or to iCloud, and are erased after 24 hours. Nothing is kept from password or card fields or forms that have them, in Private Browsing, or on sites you choose "Don't keep on this site" for. You can turn Copied off or clear it in the list or in Settings. Templates are stored in Safari's extension storage and the ContextFlow app on your device and, with "Sync chats with iCloud" on, in your private iCloud database, end-to-end encrypted like your chats.

Page watch (ContextFlow 2.5 and later). When you ask ContextFlow to tell you when something on a page changes, the extension checks that page itself, from your device, while you use Safari; there are no checks while Safari is closed. Your watches, the last value seen and, for a whole page, its text are kept in Safari's extension storage on that device only (not in iCloud), and a copy of the list is shown in the ContextFlow app on the same device. When the change you asked about happens, a notification appears on that device; nothing is pushed from our server. Stop a watch at any time in the panel or in the app's list.

5. Service providers

  • Anthropic — generates answers with Claude, and checks suggested tags (ContextFlow 2.5); with your own Anthropic API key, requests go from your device straight to Anthropic (privacy policy).
  • OpenAI — generates answers when Claude is unavailable, turns message text into embeddings for suggestions (ContextFlow 2.5), and with your own OpenAI key (privacy policy).
  • Google — generates Google answers with Google Search through the Gemini API, only when you choose "Google" (privacy policy).
  • Netlify — hosts our API (privacy policy).
  • Supabase — database for counters and subscription records (privacy policy).
  • Apple — processes payments (we never see your payment details) and, with chat sync on, keeps your end-to-end encrypted chat in your iCloud.

6. Earlier versions

Versions before 2.0 asked some users to create an account with an e-mail address. Those accounts are no longer used by ContextFlow. To have such an account deleted, e-mail us from the address you used and we will delete it within 30 days.

7. Your rights

You can ask us about the data linked to your installation ID or subscription, or request its deletion, at top10resource@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (e.g. the EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA) you may have additional rights, which we honour.

8. Children

ContextFlow is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
