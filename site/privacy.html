Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · applies to ContextFlow 2.0 and later

In short: ContextFlow has no accounts and no tracking. When you ask a question, your question and the text of the page (or the text you selected) are sent to our AI provider to generate the answer and are not stored by us. Your chats stay on your device.

1. Who we are

ContextFlow ("we") is a Safari extension with a companion app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Contact: top10resource@gmail.com.

2. What we process and why

3. What we don't do

  • No accounts, e-mail addresses or passwords.
  • No browsing history: pages are read only when you open ContextFlow and ask something.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or sharing of data for marketing.

4. Data on your device

Chats, settings, the optional "About me" text and your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key (if you add one) are stored locally in Safari's extension storage. "About me" text is included in requests to personalize answers. With your own key, requests go directly from your device to the provider of that key (OpenAI or Anthropic) under your own account with them, and do not pass through our servers. You can delete all chats in the extension settings; removing the app deletes everything.

5. Service providers

  • OpenAI — generates answers (privacy policy).
  • Anthropic — generates answers only when you add your own Anthropic (Claude) API key; requests go from your device straight to Anthropic (privacy policy).
  • Netlify — hosts our API (privacy policy).
  • Supabase — database for counters and subscription records (privacy policy).
  • Apple — processes payments; we never see your payment details.

6. Earlier versions

Versions before 2.0 asked some users to create an account with an e-mail address. Those accounts are no longer used by ContextFlow. To have such an account deleted, e-mail us from the address you used and we will delete it within 30 days.

7. Your rights

You can ask us about the data linked to your installation ID or subscription, or request its deletion, at top10resource@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (e.g. the EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA) you may have additional rights, which we honour.

8. Children

ContextFlow is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
