Text Blaze in Safari: what to use instead

Updated November 2026 · from the developer of ContextFlow

In Chrome you had snippets that typed a whole reply and filled in the name. Now you're in Safari, maybe on an iPhone, and they're gone: as of November 2026, Text Blaze has no Safari extension and no iPhone app. I make ContextFlow, whose templates do part of the same job in Safari, so I'm biased. Here's the honest picture.

What Text Blaze does

  • Snippets: type a shortcut and it expands into the full text, with form fields, formulas and if/then rules.
  • Values from the page: its {site} command reads the page's text, your selection, or one element picked with a CSS selector. That works only in the Chrome extension (Text Blaze's docs).
  • Where: a Chrome extension, an Edge add-on, and apps for Windows and Mac. For Safari, Text Blaze's FAQ points you to its Mac app. Mobile apps are “on our roadmap” with no date (Text Blaze's FAQ).
  • Price: a free plan with limited snippets, Pro from $2.99 a month billed yearly, and team plans with shared folders (Text Blaze's plans).

What ContextFlow does in Safari

  • A template from a reply you've already sent: select it and tap Template. ContextFlow marks what changes, like the name and the date, as blanks.
  • At the field: tap a text field, then the ContextFlow button next to it. Your templates are there, with the last five things you copied in Safari.
  • Fill from the page: the blanks come from the conversation or the page you have open, highlighted so you can check them before you send.
  • With a keyboard on a Mac or an iPad: type / and a few words of a template, then press Tab. Control-Shift-V opens the whole list.
  • Free and Pro: templates, Copied and inserting are free. Fill from the page works a few times a day on Free, and as often as you need with Pro. Templates sync through your iCloud.

How it looks for a recruiter or a support agent: replies and forms.

Where Text Blaze is better

  • Forms and logic. Text Blaze snippets can ask you for values, do the math and change with if/then rules. A ContextFlow template has blanks and nothing more.
  • Outside the browser. Text Blaze's Windows and Mac apps type snippets in any app. ContextFlow works only in Safari: not in Mail, Messages or Slack's app.
  • Teams. Text Blaze shares snippet libraries across a team, with roles. ContextFlow templates are yours, synced between your own devices only.
  • The exact same value every time. A Text Blaze selector pulls one element the same way each time. ContextFlow fills blanks with AI, so check what it filled.

Where ContextFlow is the better pick

  • On iPhone and iPad, where Text Blaze has no extension or app.
  • When the values change from message to message and you don't want to write selectors: Fill from the page finds the name, the date or the address on its own.
  • When you also want to ask about the page or draft a reply: the same button does both.

Only need fixed phrases on iPhone, in every app? Its own Text Replacement (Settings › General › Keyboard › Text Replacement) is free and may be all you need.

Questions

Is there a Text Blaze extension for Safari?

No. Text Blaze's FAQ suggests its Windows or Mac app instead. On a Mac, that app can type your snippets in Safari, but reading values from the page with {site} works only in the Chrome extension. There's no iPhone or iPad app.

Does ContextFlow work outside Safari?

No. Templates and Copied live in Safari. For fixed phrases in every app on iPhone, use Text Replacement in Settings › General › Keyboard.

Where are my templates kept?

On your devices, and in your iCloud if sync is on. When you tap Fill from the page, the template and the page's text go to my server to find the values. Nothing is kept there.
