Leaving Arc or Chrome for Safari

Updated November 2026 · from the developer of ContextFlow

Arc is shutting down, or you switched to Safari, or you read on an iPhone, and a tool you had isn't there. I make ContextFlow, a Safari extension for iPhone, iPad and Mac, so I'm not neutral. What's the same on every page

  • One extension: where ContextFlow fits, it does all of these inside Safari, so there's one thing to turn on.
  • Safari only: it doesn't work in Chrome, in other browsers, or in other apps like Mail and Messages.
  • No account: nothing to sign up for. Chats and templates stay on your devices and sync through your iCloud.
  • Free to start: Pro and Ultra add more answers and more pages watched.
